Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

At QuickFreeTools, we value your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

You can use many of our online tools without creating an account. If you contact us through our contact form, we may collect the name, email address, subject, and message you submit. This information is used to review and respond to your enquiry.

2. How Our Tools Handle Your Files

Our image tools are designed to process images in your browser where supported. Files selected for these tools are not intentionally uploaded to our server by the page code. Avoid submitting sensitive or confidential files to any online service unless you understand how it works.

3. Cookies and Analytics

QuickFreeTools may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, traffic measurement, and advertising if these services are enabled. Third-party providers may use their own technologies according to their privacy policies.

4. Advertising

If we display advertisements, including Google AdSense ads, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalise ads, subject to your settings, location, and applicable law. We will update this policy as needed when advertising services are enabled.

Learn how Google uses information on partner sites at Google's partner sites policy.

5. How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form may be used to respond to questions, investigate reported problems, improve our tools, and prevent spam or misuse. We do not ask you to submit passwords or payment-card details through the contact form.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services for hosting, form submissions, analytics, or advertising. These services may process information under their own policies. We encourage you to review the policies of services you use.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted to us. Contact messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, maintain records, and address misuse. No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

QuickFreeTools is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted. You may also be able to manage cookies through your browser or available consent settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website, tools, and services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use our Contact Us page.
